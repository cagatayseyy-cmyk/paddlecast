Privacy Policy

Effective date 17.11.2026

This policy explains how Seyapps, an individual developer based in Türkiye, handles data for Paddlecast (com.seyapps.paddlecast).

Data we collect

  • Game save. Progress, inventory, in-game balances, preferences and your age-band answer are stored on your device. For players aged 13 or older, the save, including these fields, is sent to Unity Cloud Save when online sync runs.
  • Player ID and nickname. Unity Authentication creates a player ID for online features. Your nickname or a generated default name may sync to that account. Leaderboard score submissions are disabled in this version.
  • Purchases. The App Store or Google Play processes payment. The game receives product and transaction details and a receipt from the store to deliver purchases and restore eligible items. Completed transaction IDs may be included in the game save.
  • Notifications and analytics. With permission, reminders are scheduled on your device. The game's analytics event reporting is disabled.

Third parties

  • Unity provides player sign-in and cloud sync. Remote Config retrieves online game settings.
  • Google serves AdMob ads and processes Google Play purchases on Android.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases on iOS.

Advertising and permission choices

Google AdMob may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. Google may use IP addresses to estimate general location. When available, open Settings → Ad privacy to revisit the ad privacy form. You can change app tracking permission in iOS settings and advertising ID preferences in Android's ad settings. Manage notification permission in device settings.

Children

The game asks which age band applies to you (under 13 or 13 and older). The game skips online sign-in and ad initialization for players who select under 13. The purchase flow includes a parental approval step.

Data retention and deletion

To start fresh on your device, use Settings → Reset data. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your cloud save and the online player account, then resets the device save. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email. Stores may keep transaction records under their own retention policies. See the account and data deletion steps.

Your rights

Under Türkiye's KVKK and, where applicable, the GDPR, you can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data and may object to processing in certain circumstances. Send your request to the address below.
